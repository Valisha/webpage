Join Mass Innovation Nights to celebrate local innovators and the Museum of Science's Year of Revolutionary Science!

Local Boston-area startups showcase new products in front of a live audience, and attendees vote for their favorite. The winning product gets to present at the event. This is a great opportunity to see what's new from the region's startup community and connect with founders, builders, and fellow innovation enthusiasts.

This event is open to anyone interested in local innovation and supporting Boston-area startups.

## Logistics

- **Tickets:** Free, available on the Museum of Science's website.
- **Voting:** Visit the Mass Innovation Nights website ahead of time to vote for your favorite product.
