## Details

Few books change how a room full of data scientists sees the world quite like this one. Join Boston Women in Bioinformatics for a virtual discussion of [_Invisible Women: Data Bias in a World Designed for Men_](https://share.google/BduErWq4OKtKluS7M) by Caroline Criado Perez — a look at how gender data gaps shape everything from medicine to city planning to the tools we build every day.

We're reading it now because a lot of the premises behind this year's [Fall Fundraiser](/events/fall-fundraiser-2026) trace directly back to the arguments in this book. Coming to the discussion first will give you the background for those conversations.

### What to Know Before You Join

- Listening to the audiobook counts as "reading."
- "Readers" are welcome even if you haven't finished the book yet — some of the facts in here are emotionally taxing and take time to digest.
