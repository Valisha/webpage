## About Event

Join the Boston Area Group for Informatics and Modeling (BAGIM) at Democracy Brewing for a partner networking happy hour hosted alongside Revvity Signals and 20/15 Visioneers. Connect with fellow computational chemists, modelers, and informaticians across the biotech and pharma industries while enjoying complimentary food and drinks.

## About Bioinformatics & AI XChange

[BAGIM](https://www.meetup.com/boston-area-group-for-informatics-and-modeling/) is an active Boston-area community that unites scientists from diverse modeling and informatics fields to share ideas, discuss research, and network across academia, government, and industry.
