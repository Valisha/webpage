## Don't Lose Us in Spam

To ensure you never miss an update, add **newsletter@boston-wib.org** and **communications@boston-wib.org** to your email contacts.

For guaranteed delivery, click below to see specific steps for your provider:

<details class="image-description">
<summary>Instructions</summary>

### Gmail (Desktop)

1. Click **Settings (Gear icon)** > **See all settings**.
2. Go to **Filters and Blocked Addresses** > **Create a new filter**.
3. In the **From** field, paste: `newsletter@boston-wib.org OR communications@boston-wib.org`
4. Click **Create filter**, check **Never send it to Spam**, and click **Create filter** again.

### Outlook (Web)

1. Click **Settings (Gear icon)** > **Mail** > **Junk email**.
2. Under **Safe senders and domains**, click **Add**.
3. Type `boston-wib.org` and press **Enter**.
4. Click **Save**.

### Apple Mail (iPhone/Mac)

1. Open the **Contacts** app and click **+** to create a new contact.
2. Name it **Boston WIB**.
3. Add both email addresses to the contact card and save.

</details>

_Tip: If an email is already in your Spam folder, open it and select **Not Spam** or drag it to your Primary inbox to train your provider._

### Fall Fundraiser

        <li>Eli Lilly Gateway Labs, Seaport</li>
        <li>
          <a
            href="https://boston-wib.org/events/fall-fundraiser-2026?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=fall-fundraiser-2026"

As scientists, we often describe bioinformatics as objective. But every dataset, every pipeline, and every model is shaped by human decisions. For much of history, those decisions have been primarily driven by male perspectives. At our Fall Fundraiser, female senior leaders in our field will lead us in answering a powerful question: **If the female perspective had been fully represented throughout the history of biomedical science, what would we know today that we don’t know now?**

The panel will explore this question through the lens of women’s health, from the long-standing gaps in research to the ways women’s perspectives and lived experiences are helping shape the future of science.

This is one of our rare ticketed events. Ticket sales directly fund the community programming and resources we provide year-round. Want to help make it happen? We're looking for volunteers! Reach out to Sammy or Liyang on Slack, or email [events@boston-wib.org](mailto:events@boston-wib.org).

Invitations and registration opens soon so keep an eye on your inbox!

## Partner Events

**_The Semantic Layer for Life-Science AI: Building Agents and Apps on the Amass API_**  
Bioinformatics and AI XChange | Virtual | Aug 22, 10:00 AM  
Join Bioinformatics & AI XChange for a hands-on, demonstration-focused session with Alexander Junge, co-founder and CTO at Amass. Junge will introduce Amass, a unified API integrating biomedical publications, clinical trials, regulatory documents, therapeutic molecules, and gene targets. The session covers how to build life-science AI agents that access structured, cross-linked biomedical knowledge through a single API. More information [here](https://boston-wib.org/events/2026/20260822_bxc_event?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=bxc-event).

**_Women's HealthX Conference_**  
Women's HealthX (WoHX) | Encore Boston Harbor, Boston, MA | Dec 3–4  
Women's HealthX is a two-day conference for the leaders building the women's health ecosystem from the ground up with sex-specific data, longitudinal research, and measurable outcomes that influence policy, reimbursement, product development, and clinical practice. You can find more information about tracks and registration [here](https://www.alphaevents.com/events-whx). There will be limited opportunities through BWIB for student tickets–stay tuned!
